Frequently Asked Questions

A little more clarity

Answers to common questions about getting started, our services, and investing with care.

01Are there shortcuts or guaranteed ways to get rich overnight?

No. Promises of overnight wealth are unrealistic. Long-term financial goals are better approached with informed decisions, discipline, patience, and consistency—not quick-rich schemes. 📈

02Is the first consultation free?

Yes. The initial consultation is free and gives us a chance to understand your goals and questions, with no obligation to proceed.

03Will I be pressured to buy a product?

No. Our aim is to explain available options clearly so you can make informed decisions without pressure.

04Why choose InvestMart?

We focus on financial clarity, goal-based planning, transparent guidance, and helping you understand options relevant to your objectives.

05Who can work with InvestMart?

Beginners, salaried professionals, business owners, and people planning for retirement can all benefit from clear, structured financial education and guidance.

06Can beginners get started?

Yes. We explain financial concepts in approachable terms and help you understand the steps and questions to consider as you learn.

07What investment topics do you cover?

We provide information about equity, mutual funds, bonds and fixed income, insurance planning, and portfolio allocation. Mutual fund distribution is provided under AMFI ARN-358551. NISM credentials do not mean SEBI registration as an Investment Adviser.

08How do you assess what may suit my goals?

Educational discussions can consider factors such as your stated goals, time horizon, risk comfort, and financial situation. InvestMart is not a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser and does not provide personalized investment advice.

09How much do I need to start investing?

There is no single amount that suits everyone. It depends on the product, your budget, and your goals. Review product documents and applicable minimums before investing.

10Is investing risky?

Yes. Investments carry risks, including possible loss of capital. Understand the risks, product documents, and your own comfort level before making a decision. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

11How often should my portfolio be reviewed?

Review frequency depends on your goals and circumstances. Periodic reviews can help you check whether your plans and investments still reflect your needs and risk comfort.

12Is ongoing support available?

Yes. You can contact us for continued discussions and financial education as your questions and circumstances change.

13What makes your approach different?

We emphasize education, research, transparency, and disciplined long-term thinking rather than chasing trends or pressuring people to buy products.

14How do I get started?

Arrange a free initial consultation, share the questions you would like to discuss, and we can outline possible next steps. Contact InvestMart to begin. 🚀

15Can I ask about investments I already hold?

Yes. We can discuss general portfolio concepts and help you identify questions to consider. This is educational information, not personalized investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell a specific security.
